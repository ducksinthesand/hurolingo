# Insult Catalogue — Offline Master

> Source of truth is `src/data/*.json`. This file and `supabase/seed.sql` are generated from it — regenerate with `npm run generate` after editing.

**Total entries:** 280 across 12 languages.

## Content rules applied

All entries target universal, changeable traits or are general-purpose profanity. Terms that function as slurs against ethnicity, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, appearance, or any protected group were deliberately excluded — including several that are statistically "common" in their languages. Severity: 🟢 mild · 🟡 medium · 🔴 strong/explicit.

## Categories

- 🎈 **Playful & Silly** — Harmless teasing — safe for almost anyone
- 😌 **Safe in Public** — Mild enough for the office and grandma
- 🤝 **Best-Friend Banter** — For people who will laugh, not sue
- 🤦 **Everyday Frustration** — Traffic, stubbed toes, Mondays
- 💥 **Full Send** — Explicit. Know them — think twice before throwing them
